# Verify notes: React Native Paper example app

- The app opens on **Examples**, a scrolling list of components. Tap a row to open its screen; the back
  button in the app bar returns to the list. The drawer (menu icon) holds theme and preference toggles.
- **Button**: the top "Playground" section shows a "Play me" button with Mode, Size and Shape options
  and switches (Show icon, Disabled, Loading) below it. Further down: Modes, States, Size, Shape, Toggle.
- **Snackbar**: switches for Action button, Close icon button, Longer message and Longer action, then a
  "Show" button that opens the snackbar at the bottom (it hides itself after a few seconds).
- Rows further down the list (e.g. TextInput, Tooltip) need a scroll before they are visible.
